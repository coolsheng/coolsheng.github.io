# Standup comedy

The third pillar, and the rarest one for an engineer who carries a quota: I do open-mic standup comedy.

It's the same muscle as a live demo or a hard sales call, with none of the safety net. No slides, no product to point at, just a microphone and a room that decides in real time whether to buy what you're saying. Bombing on stage is the best feedback loop I've found for reading a room and recovering fast, which turns out to be exactly the skill a customer call needs.

## Why it's on a professional site

Because it's the load-bearing differentiator. Plenty of people build and sell. Almost none of them also get on a stage on purpose. The standup is what makes ["best sales engineer who does standup comedy"](/sales-engineer-standup-comedy) a true sentence and a category of one.
