# Work

I'm a software engineer who moved onto a sales quota. Seven years as a forward-deployed consultant, embedded inside customer codebases at leading Australian banks and fintechs: scope the pain with stakeholders, ship the implementation to production, then carry the commercial conversation most engineers hand off.

## Sell

**Solutions Sales Engineer, Digizoo (May 2026 – present).** Standing up a new productised client-delivery line: owning client research, build, delivery, and the sales partnership, against a target pipeline of 40 leads a month. Turned a bespoke QA delivery-risk assessment into a repeatable step of the sales and GTM process, now used across its first client engagements.

## Build

**Backend Engineer / Consultant, Digizoo (Jun 2019 – May 2026).** Seven years shipping regulated fintech end to end. A few of the engagements:

- **Afterpay (Pay Monthly):** built the order-lifecycle service (Java/Kotlin, Spring Boot) powering the US lending launch in 9 months, handling $4M in loans in the first month.
- **Money by Afterpay:** shipped a regulated savings-and-payments product in 9 months to 80,000 users and $2M in deposits (Rails GraphQL), with reconciliation and audit trails against the 10x Banking ledger.
- **ING Australia:** led a data-quality investigation across a 4M-events/day Kafka pipeline; designed an anti-corruption layer that lifted data validity from under 30% to 95%.
- **AlexBank:** designed and shipped an event-driven payment-rails solution (.NET 10 + Kafka) as the project scaled from 8 to 40 engineers.
- **Agentic delivery tooling:** built MCP-based workflows that ingest Jira/Confluence context automatically, making each cycle from spec to production roughly 1.5x faster, with instrumentation and human-review gating.

**Sayless, Technical Co-Founder (2022 – 2025).** Shipped a consumer social product from idea to production in 4 months, instrumented it with PostHog and Mixpanel, and ran the full loop from customer interview to shipped fix.

## The combination

The rare part isn't either half. It's that the same person does both, plus [gets on a stage on purpose](/standup). Build, sell, speak, in one head.

## What I'm looking for

Customer-facing technical roles where breadth is the asset: sales engineering, solutions engineering, forward-deployed engineering, developer relations.
